**Client**

Samata Health, a US scale-up that helps companies give their employees easy access to therapy and mental health support.

**The problem**

The platform built by an offshore vendor kept failing employees and employers: slow pages, missed session reminders, delayed payments. Trust was slipping, and growth was on hold.

**What we delivered**

A technical audit and documentation, backups and logging, Laravel performance optimization and rebuilt email flows, followed by ongoing support and development of the platform since 2022.

## Solution

**Heading:** Solution

### 1. Technical Audit & Documentation

A full audit of the Laravel platform: we documented business logic, architecture and weak spots, then turned them into a prioritized fix plan. The client saw the real state of the product, and every next fix became faster and safer.

**Image:** [desktop 636×509](https://www.figma.com/design/BPgoVTWRXLaYqIGFeeMwES/?node-id=3922-52445) · [mobile 319×260](https://www.figma.com/design/BPgoVTWRXLaYqIGFeeMwES/?node-id=3970-33366)

### 2. Security, Logging & Backups

Automated database backups, logging of user and system events, and security hardening around sensitive health data. Health data is protected, and the team traces incidents quickly instead of guessing.

**Image:** [desktop 636×509](https://www.figma.com/design/BPgoVTWRXLaYqIGFeeMwES/?node-id=3922-52517) · [mobile 319×260](https://www.figma.com/design/BPgoVTWRXLaYqIGFeeMwES/?node-id=3970-33392)

### 3. Performance Optimization

Step-by-step optimization of the Laravel backend, heavy database queries and Elasticsearch search, without taking the live product offline. Request time dropped from 15 s to about 1 s, a 10x boost employees feel on every visit.

**Image:** [desktop 636×509](https://www.figma.com/design/BPgoVTWRXLaYqIGFeeMwES/?node-id=3922-52647) · [mobile 319×260](https://www.figma.com/design/BPgoVTWRXLaYqIGFeeMwES/?node-id=3970-33419)

### 4. Email Flows, Payments & QA

We rebuilt email notification flows, fixed the bugs behind payment delays and added regression testing before every release. Reminders arrive, invoices go out on time, and releases don't break key user flows.

**Image:** [desktop 636×509](https://www.figma.com/design/BPgoVTWRXLaYqIGFeeMwES/?node-id=3922-52743) · [mobile 319×260](https://www.figma.com/design/BPgoVTWRXLaYqIGFeeMwES/?node-id=3972-33230)
